What makes up a monthly rate

  • Depreciation. The difference between the purchase price (after any down payment) and the Restwert. You pay for the part of the car's value you use up.
  • Interest. The cost of the money the leasing company advances.
  • Term. A longer term spreads the cost over more months.
  • Kilometerlimit. More included kilometres usually mean a lower Restwert, so a higher rate.
  • Extras. Insurance, servicing and any fees in the contract are separate costs to check.
