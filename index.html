Car leasing in Switzerland, made simple

Drive the car you want without buying it outright

Leasing means you pay a monthly rate to use a car for an agreed time and distance, then hand it back or buy it at the end. This site explains how it works in Switzerland, in plain language.

See what a lease costs and try the example calculator

Why people lease

  • Predictable monthly rate. The rate is fixed in the Leasingvertrag, so you know what the car costs each month.
  • Low upfront cost. You do not pay the full purchase price. A down payment is often optional.
  • Flexible ending. At the end of the term you can usually return the car, or buy it for the agreed Restwert (residual value).
